Which motorbikes can I rent in Pokhara?

Royal Enfield Himalayan 450, KTM 250 Adventure, KTM 390 Adventure R, Triumph Scrambler 400 XC, Royal Enfield Scram 411, Royal Enfield Classic 350, Hero XPulse 200 4V, Hero XPulse 210 and TVS Ntorq 125 scooter.

How do I book a bike?

Call or message us on WhatsApp at +977 9815139239 with the bike you want and your dates. Contact us for availability and prices.

Where are you located?

Hallan Chowk, Lakeside, Pokhara, Gandaki Province 33700, Nepal.

Can I rent riding gear from Aone Rental?

Yes. You can rent a riding jacket, riding pants, riding boots and riding gloves. Message us your sizes and dates when you book.

Can I rent in Pokhara and drop the bike in another city (one way trip)?

Yes, one way rentals are possible with a certain charge. Message us for pricing.

Do you need a deposit?

Yes, we ask for a small cash deposit. We do not keep original passports or any original documents. Message us when you book and we will confirm the deposit amount before you ride.

How well are the motorbikes maintained and how old are they?

We carry out a proper inspection and maintenance after every trip. Our own mechanic maintains all our motorbikes, and a second team double checks each bike when it comes back to our office from the garage. None of our bikes is older than 2 years for small bikes and 3 years for bigger bikes. We replace every bike once it reaches that age.
